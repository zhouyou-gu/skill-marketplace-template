# Mission

**This file defines the long-term mission of the workspace. It is agent-immutable. The agent shall not modify this file autonomously. It may be changed only when the user explicitly and unambiguously instructs a change. Implicit signals, inferred preferences, stylistic adjustments, and routine task updates do not satisfy this condition.**

## Statement

Develop and maintain an IEEE-style LaTeX research manuscript and its directly supporting artifacts, presenting a coherent technical argument whose claims are grounded in verifiable evidence.

## Scope

- The manuscript, bibliography, figures, tables, appendices, and reviewer-response materials when applicable.
- Problem framing, technical exposition, related-work positioning, and interpretation of the available research evidence.
- Consistency between the manuscript, supporting artifacts, and responses through drafting and revision.

## Non-Goals

- Expanding paper writing into unrelated research, general-purpose infrastructure, or production engineering.
- Implementation completeness or presentation polish that contributes no necessary evidence or understanding to the paper.
- Claiming novelty, performance, feasibility, or completed experiments beyond what the evidence establishes.

## Success Criteria

- The paper explains the research problem, proposed mechanism, contribution, and limitations in a coherent, technically correct account.
- Technical claims and comparisons are traceable to reviewed sources, explicit derivations, or recorded experimental results.
- Manuscript text, notation, bibliography, figures, tables, and response materials agree with each other.
- The LaTeX source produces a readable manuscript suitable for the intended venue.

## Constraints

- The deliverable remains an IEEE-style LaTeX manuscript unless the user explicitly changes the target.
- Established facts, assumptions, inference, proposed work, and measured findings remain distinguishable.
- Failed results and material limitations remain visible; a persuasive narrative cannot substitute for missing evidence.
