# Example `AGENT_GOAL.md` After Curation

```markdown
# Mission

## Statement

Formulate the core research problem for this workspace as a skill-based approach to improving agentic automated operation in reproducible research settings.

## Scope

- This workspace covers research problem framing, source-backed topic studies, and supporting notes that clarify what should be studied and why.
- In-scope work includes identifying the gap between frontier ideas and reproducible research practice, then defining how a skill-based framework could help close that gap.

## Non-Goals

- Building a product roadmap or marketplace as the primary goal of the workspace.
- Presenting speculative ideas as if they are already validated in reproducible settings.

## Success Criteria

- A clear and defensible research problem statement exists.
- The workspace identifies the missing link between frontier ideas and reproducible research practice.
- The goal file is explicit enough to guide later research planning without overcommitting to a detailed solution.

## Constraints

- Keep the statement high level until the core gap is established.
- Preserve user-approved wording unless the user explicitly requests a revision.
- Ground externally facing claims in primary or official technical sources when available.
```
